## 輸出與品質條件

```text
For standalone character assets, output true transparent alpha, not a checkerboard painted into the image and not a colored background for chroma-key removal. Keep the entire character visible with at least 8–12 percent motion-safe space around the most extended pose. No cropped ears, hands or feet. Preserve fine fur edges without white/black halos or background color spill. Request the highest available native resolution; do not describe an enlarged low-resolution source as native HD.
For pose sequences, use consistent full-body framing and identical character scale, lens, lighting and ground position in every key pose. Keep the logo and costume details stable. Use genuine intermediate poses and check motion continuity, not merely a higher frame count. Deliver transparent PNG frames and animated WebP for production; GIF preview may use a clearly stated matte background.
```
